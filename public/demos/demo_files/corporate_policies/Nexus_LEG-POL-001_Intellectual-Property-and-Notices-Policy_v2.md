## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how the Bank owns the intellectual property its people create, chiefly the software it writes, and how that work is marked as the Bank's. A notice that names the wrong owner, or none, weakens the Bank's claim to its own work and misleads whoever reads it next.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to the software, documents and other works that the Bank's staff and contractors create in the course of their work, including the work of companies the Bank has absorbed and work written with the help of an AI assistant. Third-party and open source software keeps its own notices and is used under TPR-POL-002.

## 4. Policy statements

**LEG-POL-001 clause 4.1: The Bank owns what its people create.** Software, documents and other works that staff and contractors create in the course of their work belong to Nexus Bank plc, as do the works of the companies the Bank has absorbed. Contracts with contractors and service providers assign their work to the Bank.
Traces to: none in the regulator layer (the Bank's ownership rests on the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 and on its contracts).

**LEG-POL-001 clause 4.2: Notices in the Bank's software.** Every program, script and module the Bank owns carries in its header the notice "Copyright (c) Nexus Bank plc <year first written>-<year of the latest change>. All rights reserved." and the line "Nexus and the Nexus logo are trade marks of Nexus Bank plc.", in capitals where the code is. The notice names the Bank: software inherited from an absorbed company, such as Nexus Payments Ltd (folded into the Bank on 1 April 2009), names the predecessor in its history, never in its notice.
Traces to: none in the regulator layer (the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988; the Trade Marks Act 1994).

**LEG-POL-001 clause 4.3: Third-party and open source notices.** Third-party and open source software keeps its own copyright notices and licence texts unchanged, and is used only under TPR-POL-002. The Bank's notice is never added to, or put in place of, another owner's notice.
Traces to: FCA SYSC 4.1.1R (effective processes to identify, manage, monitor and report risks).

**LEG-POL-001 clause 4.4: Work written with an AI assistant.** Software and documents written with the help of an approved AI assistant belong to the Bank and carry its notices like any other. The use of the assistant is disclosed under TEC-POL-005 clause 4.4, not in the notice.
Traces to: none in the regulator layer (the disclosure itself rests on TEC-POL-005 clause 4.4).

## 5. Governance

**LEG-POL-001 clause 5.1: Exceptions and review.** An exception to this policy needs the prior written approval of General Counsel and the Director of Risk & Compliance, and is time-limited. This policy is reviewed each year.
Traces to: FCA SYSC 4.1.1R (internal control mechanisms).

## 6. Definitions

- **Work:** software (source code, scripts, tests, configuration), documents and other material protected by copyright.
- **Notice:** the copyright line and the trade mark line that mark a work as the Bank's.
- **Absorbed company:** a company whose business and assets were folded into Nexus Bank plc, such as Nexus Payments Ltd.
