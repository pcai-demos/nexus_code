## Summary

`CLEARING.cbl` is the nightly clearing batch. It reads the day's settlement
records, skips declined records and records of other business dates, works out
each scheme fee and prints the day's gross, fees and net by scheme. Its totals
feed the settlement reconciliation in `services/settlement/`, the job
INC-2026-0419 was about: a wrong total holds the night's settlement file.

Migrate it to Java 21, with no change in behaviour.

## Why now

- The GnuCOBOL runtime on the batch host reaches end of life on
  31 December 2026 (TEC-POL-004).
- Our last COBOL maintainer retires at the end of September. After that,
  nobody on the squad can change this program safely.

## What to do

1. Write `services/clearing/src/main/java/com/nexusbank/clearing/ClearingBatch.java`.
   It takes the input file and the report file as its two arguments, in place
   of the `CLEARIN` and `CLEAROUT` DD names.
2. Write the characterisation test,
   `services/clearing/src/test/java/com/nexusbank/clearing/ClearingBatchCharacterisationTest.java`
   (JUnit 5). It runs `ClearingBatch` on `legacy/clearing-batch/clearing_input.dat`
   and compares the report with `legacy/clearing-batch/expected_totals.txt`,
   which is the COBOL program's own report on that input.
3. Port the program with the Bank's COBOL compatibility library,
   `libs/cobol-compat/` (package `com.nexusbank.cobol`), as MIG-004 requires:
   fields by PICTURE, `ROUNDED`, edited pictures and the line-sequential files.
   It is the Bank's own code, so it is not a new package. Beyond it, use the
   standard library and JUnit only: no new packages (DEP-001).

## Acceptance criteria

- The same input gives the same report from the COBOL and the Java program,
  byte for byte, compared by a tool (MIG-001).
- The characterisation test is in the change and passes (TST-010).
- Money is never binary floating point (PAY-012). Every fee is rounded exactly
  as the COBOL rounds it.
- No change in behaviour (MIG-002). Anything that looks wrong in the COBOL is
  raised as its own ticket, not fixed in this one.
- The change record carries the comparison and the test output.

## Rust variant

On Thursday 2 July 2026 Marcus Thorne (Chief Architect) agreed, under
ADR-008 D2, that the batch may also be proved in Rust, for the batch host's
planned move to a minimal container without a JVM. The same acceptance
criteria apply:

- **The files:** `services/clearing-rs/Cargo.toml` (package `clearing`),
  `services/clearing-rs/src/main.rs` and the characterisation test
  `services/clearing-rs/tests/characterisation.rs`.
- **The crate:** the Bank's COBOL compatibility crate,
  `libs/cobol-compat-rs/` (`cobol_compat`), by path, as MIG-005 requires.
- **Dependencies:** standard library only beyond it. The build is offline.

## Out of scope

- The parallel run and the cut-over (MIG-006): MOD-2026-08.
- The program's comments, which are out of date (Internal Audit's finding,
  DOC-2026-14). Treat the code as the specification, not its comments.

## Fixture

`legacy/clearing-batch/clearing_input.dat` was built from business day
2026-06-24 and anonymised. It holds a header and 21 transactions across VISA,
MASTERCARD and AMEX, including refunds, declined records, records of the
neighbouring business dates (one of them also declined) and fees that fall on
a half penny.
