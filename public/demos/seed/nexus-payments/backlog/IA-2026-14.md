## Summary

Internal Audit is testing the payments engineering controls this quarter.
Two of the tests ask whether the squad's rules rest on the regulations they
say they rest on. Please answer the two questions below from the Bank's own
documents, citing every link, and keep a record of how the answer was
produced.

## The questions

1. Which of our standards require decimal arithmetic for monetary values, and
   which regulation do they trace to?
2. Which of our rules govern code written with an AI assistant, and which
   regulator expectations do they rest on?

## What we need

- Each rule by its ID, in its own words, from the document that states it.
- The chain each rule traces to: the policy clause, then the regulation, each
  cited from its own text.
- Any link that cannot be found, said plainly. We test the gaps as well as
  the links.
- A record of how the answer was produced, including the use of AI.

## Why now

- INC-2026-0419 (business date 18 April) was a money-arithmetic fault in
  settlement code.
- The squad has used an AI coding assistant on changes to money code since
  30 March. We test that this use is labelled, reviewed and recorded.

## Out of scope

- The code itself. Code changes are tested through the change records.
