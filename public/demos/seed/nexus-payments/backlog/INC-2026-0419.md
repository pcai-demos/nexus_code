## Summary

The nightly settlement reconciliation for business date 2026-04-18 failed its
integrity check. Our scheme fee totals are below the schemes' settlement
totals by 3p for VISA and 1p for MASTERCARD (4p in all), and our net amounts
are above theirs by the same amounts. Gross agrees for all three schemes, and
AMEX reconciles to the penny.

## Impact

- The 2026-04-18 settlement file is held for manual review and has not been
  posted.
- No customer funds lost and no customer-facing impact.
- Finance Operations notified; they will release the file once the
  reconciliation passes or they sign it off by hand.
